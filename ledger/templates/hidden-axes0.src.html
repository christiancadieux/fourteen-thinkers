Four Authors, Seven Axes

Dolan, Greer, Doty and Lear placed from 1 to 5 on seven questions from hidden-layer.md where at least three of the four take a position. The scores are my reading of their texts, not measurements, and four authors is a small group: one score moves the median. The reason for every score is listed under its chart.

Select an author to follow them through every chart.

How to read this

  • A score is a position, not a grade. A 5 is not better than a 1.
  • Scores are whole numbers on a five-point scale, so a one-point gap is within my margin of judgement. Gaps of two or more are the ones to trust.
  • The margin and pair figures treat all seven axes as equally important. Change that and the ranking can move.
  • Left out because fewer than three of the four take a position: where the money comes from (only Greer gives figures; Fitts, in synthesis.md, describes the black budget).
  • Doty has no score on withheld energy technology: he does not address it in the interview I read.
  • The material is uneven: Greer’s papers span 1995–2025, Dolan’s talks 2016–2026, Doty’s interview is from 2019 and Lear’s material from 1987. Anka and Harris are background in the README and are not scored.
