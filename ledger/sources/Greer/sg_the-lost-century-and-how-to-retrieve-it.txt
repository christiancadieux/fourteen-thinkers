“The Lost Century: And How to Reclaim It” is a 2023 documentary directed by Michael Mazzola and featuring Dr. Steven Greer. Both captivating and provocative, it explores what Greer describes as a “lost century” marked by innovation, suppressed technology, and hidden truths. With a powerful narrative and rich visual storytelling, the documentary seeks to awaken public consciousness to the potential breakthroughs that may have been deliberately withheld from humanity for decades—breakthroughs that could radically transform the planet for the better.
At the heart of the documentary is the assertion that advanced technologies—such as zero-point energy and anti-gravity propulsion—have been discovered and secretly developed, yet kept from public knowledge. Greer presents a compelling argument that covert programs and private interests have prevented these innovations from being shared, primarily to protect fossil fuel industries and centralized power structures.
These suppressed technologies, he suggests, could provide clean, sustainable solutions to today’s global challenges—particularly in the realms of energy, transportation, and environmental health.

The documentary provides historical context, detailed testimonies, and a range of perspectives that reinforce the idea that this knowledge has been intentionally concealed for decades. Rather than dwelling solely on what has been lost, it encourages viewers to consider what could be gained if these hidden discoveries were brought into the light.
Greer issues a global call to action—promoting transparency, public disclosure, and the empowerment of citizens to demand access to these transformative technologies. He outlines a path forward through peaceful activism, open-source science, and spiritual awareness. The tone remains hopeful, emphasizing that the future is not lost but simply awaiting reclamation.
The documentary features insightful interviews with a diverse group of contributors, including scientists, aerospace insiders, media figures, and artists—among them Fred Durst, who provides narration. These voices collectively support its central message: humanity stands at the threshold of a technological renaissance—one that could deliver clean, unlimited energy and revolutionize transportation, medicine, and communication.
