The Dr. Steven Greer Podcast – Episode 22: Beliefs Drive Narratives – Catastrophic Disclosure

July 25, 2026

Facebook Twitter LinkedIn

In this wide-ranging podcast, Dr. Steven Greer provides an urgent update on the current state of UFO and extraterrestrial disclosure. He warns that the emerging disclosure narrative is being shaped around fear, national security and the idea of an extraterrestrial threat—a scenario he has described for decades as “disclosure serving secrecy.” Dr. Greer argues that this approach obscures the distinction between genuine extraterrestrial encounters, advanced human-made technologies and spiritual or transdimensional phenomena.

Dr. Greer also discusses his recent efforts to provide senior U.S. officials with briefings and supporting materials intended to correct what he believes is dangerous misinformation. He calls upon citizens worldwide to educate their political representatives, challenge fear-based narratives and support a peaceful, evidence-based approach to disclosure.

During the audience Q&A, Dr. Greer explores the transformative potential of advanced technologies in medicine, clean energy, transportation and environmental restoration. He also addresses CE5 contact, crop circles, extraterrestrial healing, the portrayal of disclosure in Hollywood, and the challenges governments face when considering the economic and social implications of releasing suppressed technologies.

Throughout the discussion, Dr. Greer emphasizes that humanity stands at a pivotal moment. The path forward, he says, requires informed citizens, responsible leadership and a higher level of consciousness—one capable of moving beyond secrecy, division and fear toward global and universal peace.
