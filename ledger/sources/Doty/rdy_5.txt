UFOs: Former spy Rick Doty’s role in a controversial case, and national security concerns

by: George Knapp

Posted: Feb 20, 2020 / 09:24 AM PST

Updated: Feb 20, 2020 / 09:25 AM PST

Rick Doty, a former Air Force special investigator, was blamed for one UFO enthusiast's problems, and unfounded rumors linger. In government intelligence circles, Doty says in an interview with George Knapp, UFOs are taken very seriously. Previously unaired. Recorded in 2019 at the UFO Mega Conference in Laughlin, Nevada.

by: George Knapp

Posted: Feb 20, 2020 / 09:24 AM PST

Updated: Feb 20, 2020 / 09:25 AM PST

SHARE

George Knapp: Well, the (Paul) Bennewitz case … you talked about that in detail, so I won’t go into too much of it. But that’s got to be on your mind for all these years. To have been blamed for driving him crazy. That’s the story. Right? Rick Doty drove that guy crazy.

Rick Doty: Yes. That was the story. That I brainwashed him, that I fed him chemicals. I mean, I’ve heard it all. We shot beams in his head. I mean, I’ve heard everything. And none of it’s true. Every aspect of that is false. Paul was just … he was so infatuated with the subject of UFOs that he did this to himself.

Knapp: He was a brilliant but troubled person.

Doty: Very brilliant, very troubled.

Knapp: He tapped into something — national security programs — that he shouldn’t have had access to that he’d somehow managed to get information about. But he also tapped into something else. He said 60% of the information he was gathering is something beyond.

Doty: Exactly.

Knapp: What is it?

Doty: We don’t know, we wish we knew. I sent that information up. There were reports made, other investigations done, scientific analysis done. They don’t know. They don’t know what these UFOs or these objects that he photographed. Or how he collected signals, especially what they were most concerned was with the frequency range. He was collecting frequencies that were up in the high gigahertz that we didn’t have at that time period in the 1980s. So it had to come from an ET source, an extraterrestrial source, not a terrestrial source.

Knapp: You were at Area 51 not once but twice, right?

Doty: Twice.

Knapp: As a security officer.

Doty: Yes. Air Force … a counterintelligence officer.

Knapp: So you were protecting legitimate national security programs out there?

Doty: Yes.

Knapp: There is an aspect of UFOs in those programs, right?

Doty: Absolutely.

Knapp: And can you … how much can you say?

Doty: Well, there’s some programs, classified programs, that dealt with satellites, photographing space-based objects. And now it could be other satellites, like Soviet satellites or Chinese satellites. But there was also an aspect of that to photograph any kind of space-based objects, or crafts, in space.

Knapp: Unknown.

Doty: Unknown crafts in space, such as UFOs, or extraterrestrial crafts.

Knapp: So that information, as well as the photographs of Paul Bennewitz that you saw that were, it’s a flying saucer. Where does that stuff go?

Doty: I wish I knew. It went to headquarters, it went to our headquarters. And from that point on, I would guess DIA has a repository someplace, and it went there. It was very difficult to get anything back that we sent up, even if we legitimately needed something for maybe a criminal case or something like that, or even this espionage case that the FBI was involved with, and they hadn’t had access to that information. So, we asked headquarters, can you send it back to us so we can brief, clear the FBI agents to be briefed into it. They wouldn’t send it back.

Knapp: Among your fellow intelligence officers and counterintelligence, is there an understanding of the phenomena or an appreciation for it? Or is that also stovepiped?

Doty: No, I’m a member of the retired intelligence officers association (Association of Former Intelligence Officers). And almost exclusively, although there’s some naysayers in a group, there’s always naysayers in every group because they weren’t given access to certain programs. But for the majority of the ones that I meet with, 30, in excess of 30 former intelligence or retired intelligence officers, they all believe this is an absolute real phenomena, UFOs, and that there’s some sort of a threat. And it varied on opinions of the different officers, but varying threats to national security, from these objects, from these crafts.
