We continue our exploration of history and present day geopolitics, this time walking amid some of the British Empire's greatest artistic treasures, historical artifacts and symbols of power - either collected, or copied, from around the known world. As luck would have it, whilst exploring Prince Albert and Queen Victoria's epic Cast Court in the V&A Museum in South Kensington, London, I ran into an old friend, Dr Peter Gandy, and together we made our way through some of the incredible scale replicas of various European architectural and sculptural masterpieces from the Classical, Medieval, Renaissance, and Gothic eras, each telling a story about successive Empires, including Trajan's Rome - each of which have since come and gone. Our conversation eventually reached the present-day global financial system which still underpins it all.
