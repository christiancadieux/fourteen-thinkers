Gloom & Doom in Iran
Fri, 25 Sep 2026
https://mearsheimer.substack.com/p/gloom-and-doom-in-iran

 On 24 September 2026, I was on “The Chris Hedges Report,” where I talked to Chris at length about why the Trump administration has no winning strategy in Iran. It certainly has no military option, which is why it is limiting itself to putting great pressure on Iran’s economy with a blockade coupled with Secretary of the Treasury Bessent’s strategy of cutting the Iranian economy off from the rest of the world. But that strategy won’t work either. And all the while, the US is pushing the international economy closer and closer to the brink.
