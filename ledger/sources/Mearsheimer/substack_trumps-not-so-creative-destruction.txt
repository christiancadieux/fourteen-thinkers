 On 15 September 2026, I was on “Judging Freedom.” The Judge and I talked about the Iran war, which is going from bad to worse for the US now that the Houthis have closed the Bab al-Mandab Strait and are destroying critical Saudi oil facilities. The flow of Saudi oil into global markets has been greatly reduced as a result, which obviously harms the international economy. Also, the Judge and I were recently in Russia together where we met and spoke with numerous Russians, including Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov. We reflected on what we learned on that trip and where we think the Russia-Ukraine war is headed.
