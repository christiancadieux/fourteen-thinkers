India’s Strategic Predicament
Thu, 10 Sep 2026
https://mearsheimer.substack.com/p/indias-strategic-predicament

 On 3 September 2026, I was on CNN-News18 in India talking about how India deals with its challenging strategic environment. India has powerful strategic and economic reasons to have a close relationship with the United States. But to the surprise of almost all Indians, President Trump has had a fractious relationship with their country. At the same time, India must deal with Russia and China, the latter of which represents the number one threat to India. Dealing with both of those great powers is not a straightforward matter, because how India interacts with them affects Delhi’s relations with the United States. On top of all this, India is a key member of both BRICS and the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO), two of the key institutions in the so-called Global South, which increasingly serves as a counterweight to the United States and the West more generally. In this show, I discuss the various problems India faces and how it is trying to deal with them.
