The path we’re on will lead to more forever wars, possibly a draft, and economic stagnation if not ruin. Joe Kent see’s a path forward, but it won’t be easy.

This is the inaugural podcast in the “Is It Too Late?” podcast series.  We begin with Joe Kent, the decorated Green Beret, CIA paramilitary officer, and former National Counterterrorism Center Director.

As you may recall, Joe Kent resigned over the Iran war, calling it contrary to US interests, driven by Israeli lobby influence rather than any imminent threat, and at the cost of American lives with no clear strategic upside or direction.

So I asked Joe, did Trump change?  Trump, for the first two to three months of his current presidency, seemed to be on track.  But then something shifted, and Trump became far more Israel-focused and placed his America First agenda on the back burner.

But our governance problems and predicaments extend far beyond any one president or party.  Sure, the government contains many decent, checklist-oriented workers and post-9/11 true believers.  Yet most institutions prioritize self-preservation, budget fights, and risk avoidance.  Over time, this becomes entrenched and self-absorbed.

Over time, a darker layer involving intelligence networks arose, as evidenced by rapid national crime-scene cleanups, blocked investigations, and Epstein pointing to an elite leverage operation involving transnational coordination.

Joe sees the two-party system functioning as a single party that consistently delivers perpetual Middle East wars, deficits, and civil-liberty erosion.

He thinks that there’s room for a new political party focused first on ending foreign wars and influence and then on the task of rebuilding America.

Is there any hope of this?  Yes, he says, especially if younger people are reached and given a clear message of what’s involved and how we get there.

But is it too late for this?  Never say never, but the hour is definitely late.
